--- SESSION: 2026-05-22 (FOLLOW-UP) ---

ACTION: Fixed index.html to correctly load external script.js.

PROBLEM: The importmap was placed in <body> after the HTML, and the <script type="module" src="script.js"> tag was also in <body>. Some browsers require the importmap to be declared in <head> before any module scripts are encountered, otherwise the bare specifier "three" cannot be resolved when the external script.js is fetched.

FIX: Moved the <script type="importmap"> block from <body> into <head>. The <script type="module" src="script.js"> stays at the end of <body> (modules are deferred by spec, so DOM is available). No changes to script.js or styles.css.
